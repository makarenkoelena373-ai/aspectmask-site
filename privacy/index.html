Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ASPECT ("we," "us," "our"), the maker of handmade designer masks and headpieces sold at aspectmask.com (the "Site"), collects, uses, and shares information when you visit the Site. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide. The Site itself does not have accounts, logins, or an on-site checkout, so we do not collect names, addresses, or payment details through the Site directly. When you choose to order a piece, you are taken to Instagram Direct (or, where offered, WhatsApp) to complete the conversation with us — any information you share there (your name, shipping address, contact details, payment arrangement) is provided directly to us through that platform and is also subject to that platform's own privacy policy.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, the Site automatically collects some technical information when you browse it: pages and product cards viewed, general device/browser type, approximate location (derived from IP address), and how you interact with the Site (for example, which product card or slide you view, or which button you tap). This is collected through the analytics and advertising tools described below.

2. Cookies & Similar Technologies

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies (such as pixels) placed by the third-party tools below to remember your visit and understand how the Site is used. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; blocking cookies may affect how some parts of the Site behave, but the Site remains usable without them.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising Tools

We use the following third-party services, which may collect information about your visit as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — helps us understand overall traffic and how visitors use the Site (pages viewed, product cards clicked, slides viewed, order-flow steps started). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel — helps us understand traffic arriving from, and measure the results of, our Instagram/Facebook content and ads, and may be used to show you relevant ASPECT content on those platforms. See Meta's Privacy Policy.

These providers may set their own cookies and process data outside of your country of residence. We do not control how these third parties use data beyond what is described in their own policies.

4. How We Use Information

  • To understand which pieces, photos, and site sections visitors find most interesting, so we can improve the Site.
  • To measure the performance of our marketing (organic content and ads on Instagram/Facebook).
  • To communicate with you and arrange an order, once you reach out via Instagram Direct or WhatsApp.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the analytics and advertising service providers named above, to the extent they collect it directly through their own tools, and as needed to fulfil an order you've placed with us directly (for example, with a shipping carrier, using the address you send us).

6. Orders, Payment & Shipping

The Site does not process payments or store payment card information — every piece is one of one, so orders are arranged individually with us via Instagram Direct or WhatsApp. We ship worldwide; any customs, duties, or import information required for shipping is handled as part of that direct conversation.

7. Your Choices & Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below. You can also limit analytics/advertising tracking by:

  • Adjusting your browser's cookie settings, or using a browser extension such as Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Managing ad preferences directly in your Meta Ad Preferences.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

9. International Visitors

The Site is available worldwide and information may be processed or stored in countries other than your own by us or by the third-party providers listed above.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Site or in applicable practices. The "Effective date" above shows when it was last revised.

11. Contact Us

For any question about this Privacy Policy or your information, message us on Instagram: @aspect.mask.
